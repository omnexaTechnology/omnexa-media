## Optimisation approach
- **Images (webp/png/jpg)** → served through `next/image` (AVIF/WebP, width-matched srcset, lazy by default; hero not affected — it is video). Originals stay in `public/assets` as the optimiser's source.
- **Videos** → not re-encoded (already 540×960 at ~0.4–1.1 Mbit/s; the only local encoder, `avconvert`, offers H.264/HEVC presets and HEVC breaks Firefox). Delivery fixed instead: `preload="none"`, source attached and playback started only when the clip is near the viewport, paused when it leaves.
